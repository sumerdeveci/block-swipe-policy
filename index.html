Support

A free puzzle game for mobile devices. Plays entirely offline.

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email tahasumer.deveci@gmail.com.

For a problem in the game, it helps to include your device model, your operating system version, and what you were doing at the time.

Before you write

Progress is saved only on your device

There is no account and no cloud save. Uninstalling the app, or resetting your device, clears your progress permanently — it cannot be recovered, and there is no way to transfer it to another device.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
